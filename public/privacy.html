Privacy Policy — AlarMates

Effective Date: November 20, 2026 · Contact: dahaprobiner@gmail.com

AlarMates ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how AlarMates collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the AlarMates Android application and website (https://alarmates.pages.dev).

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you sign in with Google or Firebase Authentication, we receive your basic profile information (name, email address, and user ID) to authenticate your account and sync Squad alarms.
  • Alarms, Tasks & Routines: Your alarms, daily tasks, subtasks, and reminder preferences are stored locally on your device. Shared Squad alarms are synced via Firebase Firestore only with members of your chosen Squad.
  • Google Tasks & Google Calendar Data (Optional): If you choose to tap Google Sync in the Tasks tab, AlarMates requests access to your Google Tasks (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks) and read-only Google Calendar events (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly) solely to import your tasks and deadlines into the App and schedule local device reminders.
  • Voice Assistant & Diagnostics: Offline voice reminders use your device's built-in Text-to-Speech (TTS) engine without sending audio off your device. If you use the online Mate AI Assistant, audio/requests are processed via our Render token server and Google Gemini Live API.
  • Advertising: Where ads are displayed, Google AdMob may process device identifiers and diagnostics to serve or measure ads.

2. How We Use Google User Data

AlarMates' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • Google Tasks and Calendar data is used only to display your tasks/events and trigger your local alarm/voice reminders inside AlarMates.
  • We do not sell, rent, or transfer your Google user data to third parties or advertisers.
  • We do not use your Google Tasks or Calendar data to train AI or machine learning models.

3. Data Storage & Deletion

Local tasks and alarms can be deleted anytime inside the App or by uninstalling AlarMates. You can revoke AlarMates' access to your Google Account at any time from your Google Account Permissions page. To request deletion of your Firebase account data, email dahaprobiner@gmail.com.
