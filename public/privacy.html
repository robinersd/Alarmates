Privacy Policy — AlarMates

Effective Date: November 27, 2026 · Version: 1.7 · Contact: dahaprobiner@gmail.com

AlarMates ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how AlarMates collects, uses, stores, and protects your information across all features of the AlarMates Android application and website (https://alarmates.online).

1. Information We Collect Across AlarMates

  • Personal Alarms, Study & Sleep Tracking: Alarms, wake-up mission preferences, Study/Pomodoro timers, manual sleep logs, and custom ringtones or wallpapers are stored locally on your device. Camera missions (Smile / Item Scan) analyze frames on-device only and never upload video. Sleep sound tracking uses your microphone locally to measure sound levels and saves only a local summary.
  • Accounts, Squads, Photos & Chat Themes: Accounts are optional for personal alarms, Study, and local Tasks. When you sign in with Google or Firebase Authentication, we receive your basic profile (name, email, UID) to authenticate you and sync Squads (up to 15 members) — including Squad messages, shared alarms, streaks, compressed custom profile photos (1x/day), Squad GC photos (1x/week), and shared chat wallpaper themes (1x/day) via Firebase Firestore. Firebase Cloud Messaging uses a device token solely to deliver alarm and chat notifications.
  • Google Tasks & Google Calendar Data (Optional): If you choose to tap Google Sync in the Tasks tab, AlarMates requests access to your Google Tasks (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks) and read-only Google Calendar events (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly) solely to import your tasks and deadlines into the App, schedule local reminders, and sync task completion or deletion back to Google Tasks.
  • Voice Assistant & Diagnostics: Offline voice reminders use your device's built-in Text-to-Speech (TTS) engine without sending audio off your device. If you use the online Mate AI Assistant, audio/requests are processed via our Render token server and Google Gemini Live API.
  • Rewards & Advertising: In-app points and unlocks are tracked for mission/streak rewards. Where ads are displayed, Google AdMob may process device identifiers and diagnostics to serve or measure ads.

2. How We Use Google User Data

AlarMates' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • Google Tasks and Calendar data is used only to display your tasks/events and trigger your local alarm/voice reminders inside AlarMates.
  • We do not sell, rent, or transfer your Google user data to third parties or advertisers.
  • We do not use your Google Tasks or Calendar data to train AI or machine learning models.

3. Sharing, Transfer & Disclosure of Google User Data

AlarMates does not share, transfer, sell, rent, or disclose your Google user data (Google Tasks or Google Calendar data) with any third parties, advertisers, analytics providers, or external AlarMates servers (including Firebase Firestore). Google API requests occur directly between your Android device and Google APIs solely to retrieve your tasks and calendar events for local display and reminders.

4. Data Protection Mechanisms for Sensitive Data

  • Encryption in Transit: All communication with Google OAuth 2.0 and Google APIs is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS / TLS.
  • OAuth 2.0 Authentication: Sign-in and permission grants use official Android Google OAuth 2.0 flows. AlarMates never accesses, collects, or stores your Google account password.
  • Local Sandboxed Storage: Imported Google Tasks and Calendar events are stored exclusively inside your device's private Android application sandbox, inaccessible to other apps.

5. Retention & Deletion of Google User Data

Imported Google Tasks and Calendar data is retained only on your device for as long as you keep the items in AlarMates; we do not store or retain any copies on external servers. You can permanently delete this data at any time by deleting tasks inside the App, clearing AlarMates' app storage in Android Settings, or uninstalling AlarMates.

You can also revoke AlarMates' access to your Google Account at any time from your Google Account Permissions page. To request deletion of your Firebase account data, email dahaprobiner@gmail.com.
